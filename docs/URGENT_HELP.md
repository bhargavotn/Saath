# Urgent help review process

Saath does not dispatch emergency services and does not infer crisis from typing, microphone, camera, or behaviour.

## What may be shown in the product

Only resources that have a named reviewer, a review date, and a stable official URL. Unreviewed regional phone numbers must not be invented or published.

Currently published:

- IASP suicidal thoughts directory: https://www.iasp.info/suicidalthoughts/
- Region: international directory
- Reviewed: 2026-11-01
- Note: this is a directory, not an emergency dispatch service.

## Review checklist before adding a regional resource

1. Confirm the organisation’s official page, not a social post or scrape.
2. Confirm the number or URL on that official page on the review date.
3. Record reviewer name, date, country/region, and language.
4. Re-review at least every 90 days.
5. Remove the listing immediately if the official source changes or the number cannot be confirmed.

No placeholder local emergency numbers are included in the interface.
